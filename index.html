About Me

Hi, I'm Kyle Lin, a student at St. Mark's School of Texas (Class of 2028). I love learning at the intersection of math, AI, and real-world problem-solving. Much of my recent work has focused on renewable energy and power systems, where I use machine learning to study load, solar, and wind behavior and better understand uncertainty and risk in power systems. I also had the opportunity to intern at the UTD AI Safety Lab, where I explored multi-agent LLM research and uncertainty quantification. Outside of research, I enjoy studying advanced math and computer science, building independent projects, and applying what I learn to real-world problems. I am active in Improv Club, which has strengthened my confidence and communication, and I also enjoy playing water polo with my teammates and friends. This site is where I share my projects, milestones, and a few fun snapshots from school life.
